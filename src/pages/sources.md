<h2 id="turnout">About the district voting data</h2>

The [representation page](/issues/representation/) uses our local analysis of Alameda County’s 2022 statement of vote. It counts valid first-choice candidate votes in the mayoral contest, excluding overvotes and undervotes. The citywide denominator is 125,401 votes.

District membership was inferred from district-Council contest precinct lists. Where precinct boundaries changed, values were allocated using polygon-area overlap. That assumes uniform voter distribution within split precincts; some underlying quantities are fractional as a result. The percentages are rounded to one decimal place.

These figures are **modeled district estimates**, rather than direct counts by current district boundaries. They show shares of mayoral votes, not turnout rates. The analysis does not identify individual voters’ race, income, homeownership, or political preferences. The estimates still need to be checked against a standalone analysis using official district boundaries before publication.

## How we use these sources

Descriptions of charter provisions link to the relevant sections so you can read the rules in context. Our conclusions about those provisions are judgments about how they would affect Oakland. Cost projections and modeled voting estimates have assumptions and limits, which we explain alongside the figures.

A vote to place a measure on the ballot does not by itself establish an endorsement, and allegations do not establish misconduct. Possible compensation changes depend on later decisions and are not guaranteed raises.
